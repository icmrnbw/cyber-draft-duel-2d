# Balance pass — 2026-10-07 (revised after QA)

Final numbers: `tools/balance_harness.gd --seeds=9` →
`builds/balance-20261008.json` (6,712 battles, 288 matches, no failures).
Baseline before any change: `builds/balance-20261007.json` (3 seeds, 2,218
battles). Earlier tuned runs (`balance-20261007-tuned.json`,
`balance-20261007-v2.json`) are superseded.

**How independent the samples are.** BattleSim's only randomness is the
Enforcer stagger roll, so a battle is almost fully determined by the two
squads, their order and the tier. Extra seeds alone replayed near-identical
fights (2 of 135 duel rows and 11–14 of 84 squad rows had any mixed result),
so raw game counts overstate the evidence. Since this revision, seeds beyond the
three historical ones also shuffle each side's deployment order, which changes
formation slots as it does in play; 20 of 84 four-unit rows now have mixed
results. Duels have nothing comparable to vary (one unit per side), and the
dense and slot panels still use only the historical seeds. Read the rates
below as ranking evidence across **28 squad pairings × 3 tiers × 2 sides**
(4-unit) and **45 duel pairings × 3 tiers × 2 sides**, not as hundreds of
independent games. Matches vary genuinely (growth offers and bot choices are
seeded): 144 per policy.

## Changes

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| Level power: +0.5 per level → ×2 per level (Lv2 2×, Lv3 4×) | Level-up was strictly worse than doubling the same group (×1.5, then ×1.33, vs ×2). A linear +1.0 fixed only Lv1→2. Now every promotion matches a doubling's raw stats and adds the tier ability; doubling keeps its extra bodies. |
| Field cap: 40 units per side, hard (`RoundState.MAX_FIELD_UNITS`) | Doubling compounds: logical squads reached 102 (fresh) and 407 (unlocked) units. Above 40, each (type, level) group keeps one body and the remaining slots go to the groups carrying the most units per body (the first version could field ~51), each carrying the strength of those it stands in for (total HP and damage per group unchanged). |
| Crowded rounds (>24 on field) show HP bars only on damaged units | At 40 per side the bars formed a solid wall. |
| Field Medic heal 38 → 34 | 38 heal × 1.3/s out-healed an Enforcer's DPS. 30 was tried first but dropped heavy_sustain from 40% to 19% in 4-unit fights; 34 brings it to 28%. |
| ArcRelay damage 15 → 17 | Control was lowest at ~33%; now 43%/53%. 19 overshot in dense fights. |

## Frame cost of crowds (desktop proxy, `tools/crowd_perf.tscn`)

Median frame time, 720×1280 window, vsync off: 12/side 0.6 ms; 50/side 2.3 ms;
100/side 12.8 ms (p95 21.6 ms) before the cap. With the cap, 200/side fields
40/side at 1.7 ms (p95 5.6 ms). Each run shows one ~0.5 s stall, likely
first-use shader compilation. **Phone frame rate is unmeasured**; a phone CPU is
several times slower than this desktop, which is why the cap is 40.

## Open design issue: Field Medic is the default fourth unit

Every named archetype except Pressure contains a Field Medic, and Pressure is
last in both panels. In the controlled-slot panel built on Cryotek/ArcRelay/
Nullbreaker, Medic is the best fourth unit (44%) and Enforcer, Demolitionist,
Bulwark and Phaseblade score 0–3%. It is not strictly mandatory: QA found that
swapping Medic out of a squad still wins in 3 of 7 tested cases. Heal tuning
alone trades this against heavy_sustain. Options for the owner: diminishing
returns on repeated healing of the same target, a non-Medic sustain source for
melee squads, or accept Medic as a core pick.
