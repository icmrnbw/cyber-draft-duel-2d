# Balance pass — 2026-10-07

Full `tools/balance_harness.gd` suite (seeds 1009/8929/16849, both sides), before
and after: `builds/balance-20261007.json` (2,218 battles) and
`builds/balance-20261007-tuned.json` (2,238 battles). No harness failures.

## Changes

| Change | Why |
|---|---|
| `RoundState.LEVEL_POWER_STEP` 0.5 → 1.0 | Level-up was strictly dominated by doubling (x1.5/x1.33 vs x2). Promotion-first player: 5/48 → 32/48 matches won vs the investment-scaled bot. Double/add player unchanged at 42/48. |
| Field Medic heal 38 → 30 | 49 HP/s out-healed an Enforcer's 40 DPS. Swapping Medic for Marksman moved a squad from 74–100% to 5–14%. |
| ArcRelay damage 15 → 17 | Control archetype lowest at ~33%; ArcRelay also weakest in duels. 17 lifted Control to ~42% without new outliers (19 overshot in dense fights). |

## Results (win rate, draws = half)

| Panel | Spread before | Spread after | Weakest after | Strongest after |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| 4-unit squads | 66 | 63 | pressure 13% | ranged_sustain 76% |
| 12-unit dense | 79 | 62 | mobility 19% | ranged_sustain 81% |

Duels are unchanged in shape (Enforcer/Trooper ~90%, Medic 0% because a support
cannot win a 1v1); duels are not used as a tuning target.

## Still open

- Pressure (Enforcer, Trooper, Demolitionist, Marksman) stays weak in 4-unit
  fights and Mobility in dense fights. Panels are 14–42 games per rate; tune one
  unit at a time with larger samples.
- Double/add player wins 87.5% against the base bot; a casual player's rate is
  unmeasured. Bot difficulty constants were not changed.
