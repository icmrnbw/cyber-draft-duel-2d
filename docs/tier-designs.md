# Tier evolution designs (draft for owner approval, 2026-10-14)

Owner direction: Lv2/Lv3 must be real evolutions, not colour swaps. Each tier
keeps the unit recognisable (palette family, helmet shape, role) but changes the
silhouette, and the change shows the tier's ability. Cyan stays the Lv2 accent
and orange the Lv3 accent so tiers still read at a glance.

| Unit | Lv2 (ability) | Lv3 (ability) |
|---|---|---|
| Enforcer | Heavy Strikes: oversized shock-gauntlets fused over both tonfas, layered riot plating on chest and forearms, T-slit visor | Berserk: hulking exo-frame juggernaut, exposed glowing reactor in the chest, horned crest on the helmet, torn half-cape, tonfas become crackling plasma batons, forward-hunched stance |
| Trooper | Burst Protocol: rifle gains a drum magazine and triple-barrel shroud, ammo-belt backpack feeding the rifle, comms antenna | Suppressive Volley: heavy assault suit, rifle becomes a belt-fed light machine gun, small hover-turret drone over one shoulder, tri-lens helmet |
| Marksman | Charged Round: sniper rifle grows capacitor rings along the barrel, small rangefinder drone at the shoulder, circuitry glowing through the cloak | Target Lock: floating ring of targeting lenses around the head, mechanical scope-eye, long tattered coat with glowing sigils, coil-charged barrel |
| Demolitionist | Firestorm: twin napalm tanks replace the single canister, launcher gains an underslung igniter, heat-shield apron | Scorched Ground: walking furnace with exhaust stacks venting flame on the back, four-barrel rotary launcher, molten cracks |
| Field Medic | Aegis Dose: second healing drone, hex-shield projector on the forearm, winged med-pack | Trauma Reset: halo of three drones, energy wing-fins behind the back, long injector staff |
| Bulwark | Aegis Ram: spiked ram-head tower shield, piston-driven hammer | Fortress Pulse: shield unfolds side wings into an energy barrier, shoulder bastion towers, glowing core |
| Phaseblade | Phase Lunge: afterimage shards trailing from the cape, blade gains a second, phase-split edge | Rift Mark: cape dissolves into void tendrils, blade fragments orbit the body, cracked mask leaking orange light |
| Cryotek | Cryo Lock: ice crystal growths on shoulders and tanks, cannon gains a frost compressor | Thermal Collapse: glacial titan with massive crystal pauldrons, frost aura, cannon becomes a heavy cryo-lance |
| ArcRelay | Arc Chain: backpack relay pylons extend arcing antennas | Signal Jam: relay nodes float and orbit, staff fork becomes a three-prong lightning crown, coat flares open |
| Nullbreaker | Piercing Charge: cannon gains an extended null barrel with stabiliser fins, shoulder capacitor | Expose Core: exposed core harness on the chest, cannon splits into twin rails, hood with a single void eye |

Pipeline once approved: one new 4-frame idle sheet per unit tier (20), then
attack/walk/retreat per unit tier generated with the new idle attached (60),
sliced with the idle scale/foot lock.
