# Ten-unit balance measurements — 2026-10-04

This is an initial measured tuning pass, not final balance approval. Reports were
produced by the installed Godot 4.7.1 executable and loaded shipped resources.
All growth/unlocks in the harness are in memory; PlayerProfile is never changed.

## Method

`tools/balance_harness.gd` uses seeds 1009, 8929 and 16849, with each pairing
reversed across sides. Equal-tier battles use RoundState's real power multiplier.

- 810 duels: all 45 distinct unit pairs, three tiers, three seeds, both sides.
- 504 four-unit battles: all 28 pairs of eight named legal archetypes, all tiers,
  three seeds, both sides.
- 168 dense battles: three copies of each drafted unit (12 per side), all tiers,
  all 28 archetype pairs, one seed, both sides.
- 336 controlled-slot battles: two fixed three-unit cores, each of seven legal
  fourth units, three tiers, four opponents and both sides, one seed.
- 96 full progression matches: eight archetype pairings, fresh/unlocked modes,
  three seeds and both human sides. Match safety exhaustion fails the harness.

Support-only duels are not a measure of support value. The dense and slot panels
are coarse one-seed samples. Draws count as half a win in rates below.

## Baseline

`builds/balance-baseline-v2-20261004.json` / matching `.log`: **2,219 battles**
including rounds from **96 completed matches**, 199.90 seconds, no harness
failures and no battle timeout in the fixed battle panels.

The earlier `balance-baseline-20261004` run is incomplete: a typed-array error
skipped the dense panel even though Godot exited 0. Do not use it as a full-suite
pass. The corrected v2 run contains every panel.

Clear outliers before tuning:

- Control archetype won 0% of Lv3 four-unit games and 14.3% of Lv3 dense games.
- Mobility won 100% of Lv1 four-unit games, but 0% of Lv2/Lv3 dense games.
- Area sustain won 92.9% / 100% / 92.9% of dense games at Lv1/Lv2/Lv3.
- Enforcer and Trooper each scored 94.4% in Lv3 duels; this is not enough by
  itself to justify a nerf because squad/role results differ.

## Small control adjustment

Changed only ArcRelay base damage **13 -> 15** and Cryotek base damage **16 -> 18**.
No attack intervals, range, targeting or ability gates changed. This raises the
weak control core's ordinary pressure while retaining its group-control role.

The exact same squad/dense/slot panels were rerun: **1,008 battles**, 98.23 seconds,
no harness failures (`builds/balance-control-tune-20261004.json` and `.log`).
The 327-check ability suite also passed after this adjustment.

| Control archetype | Before | After | Games per rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Four units, Lv1 | 28.6% | 42.9% | 42 |
| Four units, Lv2 | 23.8% | 31.0% | 42 |
| Four units, Lv3 | 0.0% | 28.6% | 42 |
| Twelve units, Lv1 | 28.6% | 35.7% | 14 |
| Twelve units, Lv2 | 42.9% | 57.1% | 14 |

Retained this modest improvement. It does not solve the high-tier control deficit
or dense area-sustain dominance. Larger samples, additional squad contexts and
further measured iterations remain necessary.

## Progression caveat

The baseline fresh policy prioritizes double then add; it won 40/48 matches
(83.3%). The unlocked policy deliberately prioritizes every available level-up,
then double/add, against the existing maximum bot difficulty. It won only 5/48
(10.4%), with 285 promotions. Both completed without match draws.

These are different decision policies and different bot difficulty settings;
they do **not** prove that unlocking tiers makes an optimal player weaker.
The result exposes an unresolved interaction between duplication, promotion
value and bot difficulty. Compare equal decision policies and individual
promotion value before changing progression/difficulty constants. No such
constants were changed in this pass.

The post-adjustment progression rerun completed another **96 matches / 400
round battles** in 86.66 seconds with no failures. Fresh: **42/48 human wins
(87.5%)**; unlocked promotion-first: **5/48 (10.4%)**, 288 promotions, no match
draws. This retains the policy/difficulty concern rather than resolving it.
Evidence: `builds/balance-progression-after-20261004.json` and `.log`.
The harness now checks each panel's expected row count, so a nested script error
cannot silently skip a whole panel and still produce a successful report.

## Commands

From the project directory, with `$godot` pointing to the installed
`Godot_v4.7.1-stable_win64_console.exe`:

```powershell
& $godot --path . --headless --log-file builds/balance-baseline-v2-20261004.log --script res://tools/balance_harness.gd -- --report=res://builds/balance-baseline-v2-20261004.json
& $godot --path . --headless --log-file builds/balance-control-tune-20261004.log --script res://tools/balance_harness.gd -- --suite=squads --report=res://builds/balance-control-tune-20261004.json
& $godot --path . --headless --log-file builds/balance-progression-after-20261004.log --script res://tools/balance_harness.gd -- --suite=rounds --report=res://builds/balance-progression-after-20261004.json
```

Reports intentionally refuse overwrites; use new output names when rerunning.
